How I work

01

I'm a front-end developer who ends up building more than front ends. Most projects start as a browser interface and then grow a second half — a CLI, a desktop shell, a background service — because the interesting part of the problem was never just the layout.

Recent work spans client sites and challenge builds, alongside Electron apps and Node tools built for my own workflow: a queue-based download manager, a CLI wrapper around it, and a couple of small Windows utilities that started as "I wish this existed."

I care about interfaces that respond precisely — correct states, accessible focus, motion that means something — and about the plumbing behind them being just as considered.
